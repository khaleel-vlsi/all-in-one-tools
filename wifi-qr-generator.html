WiFi QR Code FAQ

How do I connect using a WiFi QR code?

Open your phone camera or QR scanner and point it at the downloaded card. Supported devices will offer to join the network.

Is my Wi-Fi password uploaded?

No. The QR code is generated locally. Your password is included in the QR code, so share the downloaded card only with people you trust.

Which encryption type should I choose?

Choose WPA for most modern home and business networks, WEP only for older routers, or None for an open network without a password.

Can I use a hidden network?

This simple generator creates a QR code for a visible network. Hidden network settings may need to be entered manually on some devices.

Share Wi-Fi simply

Print the downloaded card or display it on a screen. Your password is encoded in the QR, so keep the card private.
