JPG to 50KB Compressor FAQ

How do I compress a JPG or PNG to 50KB?

Select an image, enter 50 in the target size field, and choose Compress Image. The tool searches image quality and dimensions locally to get the file at or below the target when possible.

Does this tool upload my image?

No. Your image is decoded and compressed in your browser with HTML5 Canvas. It is not sent to a server.

Why might the result not be exactly 50KB?

Image encoders produce discrete file sizes, so an exact byte size cannot always be achieved. The tool aims for a result at or below your selected target and reports the actual size.

What happens to transparent PNG areas?

The output is a JPEG, which does not support transparency. Transparent areas are composited onto a white background.
