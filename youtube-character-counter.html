YouTube Character Counter FAQ

What is the YouTube title character limit?

YouTube titles can contain up to 100 characters. The title meter turns red when your text exceeds that limit.

How long can a YouTube description be?

The description limit is 5,000 characters. Paste or write your description to see the live count.

How many hashtags can I add to a YouTube video?

YouTube allows up to 15 hashtags associated with a video. This checker counts whitespace-separated words that start with #.

Does this checker save or upload my text?

No. The counters run locally in your browser and do not send your title, description, or hashtags anywhere.
